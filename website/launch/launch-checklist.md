# Public launch checklist

The source and working preview are built. None of the steps below are represented as completed external actions.

1. Confirm a new hosting project or provide the intended existing site. Do not replace another business's site.
2. Publish the static build under an actual origin. Set SITE_URL and confirm real canonical URLs and sitemap.
3. Load the home page, a destination, a field guide and the planner directly on desktop and iPhone Safari. Verify no 404s or horizontal overflow.
4. Update a plan, reload it, change destination, reload again, and verify native browser storage. Test a shared snapshot in an unrelated browser session.
5. Test native share/clipboard, print and text download on actual devices.
6. Add the owner's actual Amazon tag. Inspect outbound links and only claim commissions are active after account configuration is confirmed. Replace search links with specifically reviewed items where possible.
7. Enable and verify hosted operator-interest forms before setting ATLAS_FORMS_ENABLED=true. Send a clearly marked test submission and verify it is visible to the owner. Do not claim email notification or CRM delivery without connecting and testing it.
8. Attach an actual analytics collector and verify attribution plus plan, shopping, sharing and operator events. Keep personal details out of event properties.
9. Review starter guides and independent operator links. Do not label listings “partners” or publish availability/prices without confirmation.
10. Begin small, personal operator outreach and original trip-content distribution. No mass mailing, ad spend or operator contact is authorized by the source package itself.

Native bookings, payment milestones and actual escrow remain a separate implementation, not an enabled feature of this release.
