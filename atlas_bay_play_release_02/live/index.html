ATLAS BAY / PLAY LAB 02

A more tangible cockpit.
A more believable coast.

Explore: click a lid, tackle drawer or front seat bolster. Hatch handles release before their lids rise; the fish-box strut telescopes. All three drawers have modeled trays and tackle. Lights and bolster positions are independent.

Drive: start in neutral with the throttle at idle. Select Forward after startup, then add throttle. W/S changes throttle; A/D steers; F/N/R selects a gear. E toggles the engine. Q/Z trims out/in. Space selects neutral; it does not stop the boat. Coasting and current continue to move the hull.

Conditions: switch between Calm, Sea breeze and Light chop. They alter the shared water-wave field, current and windage. Ground speed can differ from speed through water. The depth display and islands belong only to this fictional basin.

Small mechanisms: drawers and lids close before propulsion is enabled in Drive mode. Wheel, throttle, outboard and instruments follow the controls. Return to Explore to inspect storage. The Pause button freezes the simulation; it is not a real boat control.

Trim: the entire outboard rotates at its pivot. Trim influences the game's bow attitude and thrust efficiency. This is a qualitative illustration, not engine or hull calibration. High-speed and shallow-water limits are not demonstrated.

Audio: Sound is opt-in. The engine, water and latch sounds are synthesized; they are not recordings of a Yamaha or other specified engine.

Rendering: Detail: smooth reduces shadows and pixel density without replacing the boat. Hide panels gives a clean view. Escape restores panels. Portrait controls scroll within the lower panel.

Limits: force coefficients, RPM, planing transition, wave response, trim, collisions and a 0.70 m fictional ground-contact trigger are game-tuning choices. None establishes actual mass, stability, horsepower, draft, seamanship or safe operation. A 450 hp engine is not fitted. The model remains a standalone Bay; Explorer's garage is unchanged.

Free walking, full fishing gameplay, physical-device testing and offline/app-store packaging are not included in this release.
