Four original identities, one selected vessel. This is an authored offshore simulator—not a real chart, sea-trial-calibrated ship or passenger-certified submarine.

Navigation that explains the motion

A north-up depth chart, up to eight manually plotted waypoints, bearing/range, cross-track error, estimated arrival, travelled track, and forward clearance warnings. Speed through water differs from speed over ground when current flows. Course is unavailable at near-zero ground speed; ETA is unavailable when not closing on the waypoint. You remain in control of steering.

A physical environment, not a faster clock

Earth-fixed current acts through water-relative motion. Apparent-wind loading acts on the exposed craft and fades underwater. Submarine descent uses a force-limited buoyancy actuator with lag, added vertical mass and drag. Hull-scale wave response samples 21 points; rendered and sampled waves share phase and analytic normals. All parameters are published assumptions, not measured hydrodynamics.

More believable materials and water

Clear-coated yacht paint; matte naval paint and submarine rubber; finer teak, metal and upholstery separation; a transmissive canopy with additive hardware on Kayley’s blue pod. Wave detail, outdoor reflections, path-following foam and underwater color absorption are rendering approximations. Visual quality and functional browser acceptance must be checked separately from numerical tests.

Boundaries

Not full six-degree-of-freedom hydrodynamics, CFD, real measured tank filling, collision certification, live ocean data, multiplayer or human occupancy. The original models and previous releases are unchanged. The Beaufort experience remains a separate linked app.
