ATLAS D / SUMARS

Take the helm.

Start propulsion in neutral, choose F, then add power. Hold Port/Starboard to turn. Idle removes thrust, not momentum; reverse thrust slows a moving vessel.

Keyboard: E engine · F/N/R gear · W/S or ↑/↓ power · A/D or ←/→ steering · P pause · Space idle. Drag the ocean to orbit; scroll to zoom.

ATLAS-D must be nearly stopped, in neutral and idle before Secure yacht. After the original exterior closes, choose a depth and Dive. ATLAS-N and SUMARS are separate submarines and need no yacht conversion. ATLAS-W stays on the surface.

Navigation: open Chart & route and tap the water to add waypoints. Bearings, distance, ETA and projected clearance are advisory. You control the helm. SOG is speed over ground; speed through water differs when a current is active. The chart is an authored scenario, not real geographic data.

One vessel is active at a time. Selecting another launches it at the safe origin; the previous craft is removed. A hidden tab or long render stall pauses; losing focus idles inputs. Recovery is deliberate.

This is a solo concept simulation in authored offshore water—not Beaufort chart data or a certified vessel. Force/inertia parameters and SUMARS geometry are authored assumptions; depth now integrates a bounded vertical control force and drag, but is still not a full ballast/pressure-hull model. No human occupancy, combat systems or multiplayer. The Beaufort link is a separate experience.
