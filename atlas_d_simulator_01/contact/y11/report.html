Measured in simulation—not sea-trial validated. The original 82.9 m yacht geometry is preserved. Mass, added mass, damping, thrust, rudder coefficients and restoring periods are authored assumptions. These are not certified real-vessel specifications, and no pressure-hull rating is claimed.

What changed

Direct target-speed interpolation was replaced with a 3-degree-of-freedom force model: surge, sway and yaw inertia, energy-consistent Coriolis coupling, linear/quadratic damping, distributed hull crossflow, rudder side force and bow-thruster moment. Thrust and rudder actuators respond gradually. Heave, roll and pitch use separate damped restoring equations instead of immediately copying wave slopes.

The same owner 60 Hz simulation step drives the model; there is no second vessel clock. Original input handlers, conversion sequence and the >4-second stall pause remain. Unknown bottom data remains unknown. The original 39-point footprint gains an additional conservative tilt allowance. Depth control is still the bounded inherited concept controller, not a validated ballast or submarine dynamics model.

Identical scripted maneuvers

Draft datum 4.507 m; flat 200 m-deep synthetic test water; calm waves; 1/60-second step. Acceleration begins at rest with full forward command. Coast, stopping and turning start after 720 seconds of full-forward equilibration. Full reverse means immediate command reversal with physical speed continuity. These are not regulatory trials or pass/fail maneuvering standards.

Numerical and safety checks

26 focused checks pass. They test mathematical consistency and stated safeguards, not whether the authored parameters match a real yacht.

  • Zero force / still water preserves equilibrium
  • Added masses and yaw inertia are positive
  • Unforced Coriolis plus hull/rudder damping dissipates energy
  • Numerical unforced kinetic energy falls monotonically
  • Full-thrust speed matches analytic force-balance root
  • Thrust follows the specified time constant
  • No speed or yaw teleport at control changes
  • Rudder respects 5-degree-per-second actuator rate
  • Unpowered stationary rudder cannot turn the hull
  • Positive steering turns starboard and develops sideslip
  • Port/starboard maneuver responses are mirror symmetric
  • Bow thruster acts without forward motion and is engine gated
  • Wave motion has lag rather than an algebraic pose jump
  • Observer calls do not advance vessel/oscillator/actuator state
  • Pause freezes motion, wave response, conversion and thrust
  • Dive requires original exterior closure
  • Partial unknown footprint refuses dive
  • Shallow forward footprint refuses target-depth dive
  • Tilt reduces reported bottom clearance conservatively
  • Large lateral drift blocks securing/deployment
  • Original conversion duration retained without world descent
  • Dive, hold, surface and deploy preserve operating interlocks
  • Unknown coverage interrupts descent and stops submerged translation
  • Ground contact idles force and translational/yaw velocities
  • Rejected inputs and reset remain finite/versioned
  • 60Hz and 120Hz reference trajectories converge

Model basis and limits

Fossen: marine craft equations, mass, Coriolis and damping; Fossen: hydrostatic restoring motion. Our simplified rudder model is not MARIN's calibrated four-quadrant model. No tank-test/CFD/sea-trial coefficients, wind/current forcing, structural damage, flooding, swept collision proof, human occupancy approval or multiplayer are included.

This is isolated from CORE's integrated simulator. Phone-sized Chromium verification is not physical-phone/Safari or performance certification.
