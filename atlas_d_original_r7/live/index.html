WHAT THIS VERSION DOES — AND DOES NOT CLAIM

Closer to the concept,
not a safety certification.

The helipad, layered terraces, tender recovery and low stowed form follow the supplied ATLAS D concept board. The same hull and pressure-core geometry remain present throughout.

The large silhouette change uses speculative telescoping exterior sleeves. Annular deck openings reserve their travel. Upper leisure volumes are not preserved as occupied, dry pressure rooms. Structure, complete collision checks, accommodation, buoyancy and stability are unresolved.

Both tenders enter modeled aft bays before descent. These bays demonstrate packing and recovery motion—not proven protection of ordinary tenders at depth. The helipad is a visual foredeck feature, not an aviation-qualified landing facility.

The board's depth, speed, displacement and endurance numbers are not adopted. The 26 m animated descent is a prescribed display movement, not a safe operating limit.

Surface / Ready holds the same camera. X-ray is an inspection overlay, not part of the transformation. No moving hull is faded away or swapped for a different submarine.
