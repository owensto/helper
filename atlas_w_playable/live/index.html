About this rebuild

Original procedural motor yacht, with a new hull, stepped decks, bridge, pool, deck equipment and underwater running gear. This is an authored surface-yacht simulation, not measured vessel performance.

Force-based twin propulsion, thrust lag, rudder actuator and damped wave motion use disclosed concept parameters. Chart and coast are fictional. No navigation, structural, flight-deck or pressure-hull certification.

Multiplayer, walkable human contact and submarine transformation are not in this release. The original concept remains separate.
