What is measured?

The single-F300 reference reproduces Yamaha's Sportsman Masters 247 test table. It is not measured ATLAS performance. Realistic model and Sport use editable mass, thrust, drag and wave assumptions. Twins share a generic illustrative outboard mesh; mounting and hull structure are not qualified.

Yamaha source bulletin ↗

Beaufort shorelines and sparse soundings come from NOAA ENC Direct to GIS. Depth is interpolated only near soundings; missing data is not filled with fictional depth. The sonar display is simulated, not real-time sensing. Tide, waves and weather are scenarios, not forecasts.

Controls: W/S throttle, A/D steering, E engine, F/N/R gears, Space neutral, Q/Z trim, P pause, H interface, J sport hop. This app is not for navigation or seamanship instruction. Free walking, live weather/tides and scanned 3D Google scenery are not included.
