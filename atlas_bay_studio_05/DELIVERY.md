## Separate shallow-water investigation

A selectable **unvalidated shallow-bottom option** reduces aft-V depth below the existing chines. The same topsides, deck, T-top, seating and single engine remain. It is not a tunnel hull and is not the approved default. The engine installation has not been optimized or qualified for this altered bottom.

The Draft study panel shows even-keel sectional-area calculations for both geometries. It does not change the apparent waterline in the animation. No actual all-up mass, trim, stability, freeboard or engine-clearance result is established.

At 11.9 inches of hypothetical static hull immersion in seawater of density 1,025 kg/m3, the approved hull corresponds to approximately 1,114 kg TOTAL displacement mass; the optional hull corresponds to approximately 1,918 kg TOTAL. These are not payload allowances, construction weight estimates or rated capacities.

For an illustrative assumed total of 1,800 kg, interpolation gives approximately 14.7 inches for the approved hull and 11.5 inches for the optional hull. At 2,200 kg, the corresponding results are approximately 16.1 and 12.8 inches. Neither is an actual Atlas draft measurement. Everything aboard, including structure, top, motor, fuel, batteries, people, water, ice and tackle, belongs in the total mass.

The target under investigation is less than 12 inches of static HULL draft at a declared fishing load, with the outboard raised. Appendages, engine-down clearance, idling, acceleration and running depth are separate constraints. The calculation excludes trim and does not establish safe operation.
