ATLAS FLEET 01 — Visual-model production release

Five independent editable assets: ATLAS-D Explorer (R10-derived, with the utility tender replaced by Bay 25), ATLAS-N submarine, ATLAS-W surface ship, ATLAS Bay 25 rigid-T-top fishing boat, and ATLAS Chase 12.

Open the hosted index.html, then choose a model. Direct URLs use ?v=d, ?v=n, ?v=w, ?v=bay or ?v=chase. Only the selected exterior loads initially. N and W interiors load on demand.

The native Blender scenes are saved after importing the deterministic staging mesh and exporting the browser geometry. Those same native scenes are the delivery masters. Their imported hierarchy and baked rigid animations remain editable. The generator source permits regeneration, including updating the integrated Bay 25 from its canonical geometry. This is not a live cross-file Blender library link.

ATLAS-N and ATLAS-W are first complete visual models, not real platforms or engineering drawings. Internal spaces are generic inspection layouts, not naval design data. Static military exterior props have no weapons mechanisms or operation.

The yacht's speculative nesting motion is retained for visual continuity. Previous pressure, freeboard, aperture, stability and mechanism issues are not resolved by this release. Fishing-tender screening covers its complete secured geometry plus the replacement carrier against the unobstructed candidate cylindrical garage barrel. No pressure-door sweep, loads, deflections, wave motion, personnel access, wet storage, fuel-system safety or certified operating clearance has been validated.

A rigid roof remains intact. Rods, antenna, livewell lid, trolling motor and outboard have explicit securing motions. The fishing boat is a conventional surface craft, not an immersion-qualified tender.

The downloadable .blend files use metres: +X forward, +Y port, +Z up. Browser glTF uses Y-up. Cameras, lights and cutaway overlays are presentation aids. Navy handling and all underwater movement are prescribed kinematics, not real operating procedures or dynamics.

Read release_report.json, native_report.json, layer_report.json and browser validation evidence to distinguish what was built and tested from what remains provisional.
