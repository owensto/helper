ATLAS BAY / PERFORMANCE 03

Published performance.
Bounded claims.

New reference mode: forward steady speed follows Yamaha’s Sportsman Masters 247 / F300 test. Open Performance & source for the 11 measurements and repeatable tests. It is NOT a measured ATLAS result. Trim and added weather are locked in reference mode; use Experimental coastal handling to restore those controls.

Explore: click a lid, tackle drawer or front seat bolster. Hatch handles release before their lids rise; the fish-box strut telescopes. All three drawers have modeled trays and tackle. Lights and bolster positions are independent.

Drive: start in neutral with the throttle at idle. Select Forward after startup, then add throttle. W/S changes throttle; A/D steers; F/N/R selects a gear. E toggles the engine. Q/Z trims out/in. Space selects neutral; it does not stop the boat. Coasting and current continue to move the hull.

Conditions: switch between Calm, Sea breeze and Light chop. They alter the shared water-wave field, current and windage. Ground speed can differ from speed through water. The depth display and islands belong only to this fictional basin.

Small mechanisms: drawers and lids close before propulsion is enabled in Drive mode. Wheel, throttle, outboard and instruments follow the controls. Return to Explore to inspect storage. The Pause button freezes the simulation; it is not a real boat control.

Trim: the entire outboard rotates at its pivot. Trim influences the game's bow attitude and thrust efficiency. This is a qualitative illustration, not engine or hull calibration. High-speed and shallow-water limits are not demonstrated.

Audio: Sound is opt-in. Sound combines a CC0 generic outboard recording with synthesized RPM, water, wind and mechanical effects. It is not a recording of a known Yamaha engine.

Rendering: Detail: smooth reduces shadows and pixel density without replacing the boat. Hide panels gives a clean view. Escape restores panels. Portrait controls scroll within the lower panel.

Limits: outside the labeled forward reference curve, force coefficients, RPM mapping, planing transition, wave response, trim, collisions and a 0.70 m fictional ground-contact trigger are game-tuning choices. None establishes actual mass, stability, horsepower, draft, seamanship or safe operation. Selectable engine counts and horsepower are illustrative custom configurations, not approved installations. The model remains a standalone Bay; Explorer's garage is unchanged.

Free walking, full fishing gameplay, physical-device testing and offline/app-store packaging are not included in this release.

What is measured?

Sea Lab 05 uses a reference-informed resistance curve for custom-mode speed. Source measurements still belong to the Sportsman / F300 test, not Atlas. Custom engine speed and acceleration remain modeled. Speed is displayed in knots (nautical miles per hour). The new sound uses a CC0 generic outboard texture, not an exact F300/450 recording. The marine displays show the same simulated state and chart-derived depth; they are not navigation or fish-detection instruments.

The single-F300 reference reproduces Yamaha's Sportsman Masters 247 test table. It is not measured ATLAS performance. Realistic model and Sport use editable mass, thrust, drag and wave assumptions. Twins share a generic illustrative outboard mesh; mounting and hull structure are not qualified.

Yamaha source bulletin ↗

Beaufort shorelines and sparse soundings come from NOAA ENC Direct to GIS. Depth is interpolated only near soundings; missing data is not filled with fictional depth. The sonar display is simulated, not real-time sensing. Tide, waves and weather are scenarios, not forecasts.

Controls: W/S throttle, A/D steering, E engine, F/N/R gears, Space neutral, Q/Z trim, P pause, H interface, J sport hop. This app is not for navigation or seamanship instruction. Free walking, live weather/tides and scanned 3D Google scenery are not included.
