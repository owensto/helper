ATLAS BAY · PLAY LAB 01.1

Meet your boat.
Then drive it.

Explore: click the fish-box lid, port rod-locker lid or livewell cover to open and close them. The box and locker have modeled recesses beneath the lids. The buttons do the same thing.

Drive: start the engine, choose Forward, and raise the throttle. A/D or the steering slider turns the wheel. W/S adjusts throttle. N selects neutral, R reverse, F forward, E toggles the engine. Space is an arcade stop—not a real boat brake.

On a phone: use the steering buttons and throttle slider. At the helm, drag the view to look around. Select Follow to watch from behind.

Small actions: the wheel and outboard steer together; the propeller spins; the in-model displays show simulated speed and heading. Lids close and the trolling motor stows before driving. Inspect mode pauses the boat.

Not a marine simulator: speed, RPM, drag, turning, wake, bobbing and collisions are deliberately simplified. This fictional basin has no real GPS data or depths. This is not training, navigation, a horsepower rating, a draft result or a seaworthiness demonstration. Boat construction, hatch loads and full interference checks remain unvalidated. Walkthrough, fishing gameplay, audio and app-store packaging are future features—not included here.

Clear view: Hide panels removes the menus while the boat remains controllable with the keyboard. Show controls or Escape restores them.

Graphics: Detail: smooth reduces pixel density and dynamic shadows without removing boat geometry. Detail: high restores the richer lighting.
